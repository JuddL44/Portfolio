About Me

Software Development

Nine years ago, I wrote my first line of code and never looked back. What began as a passion

for game development evolved into a professional career, earning a place in Mojang

Studios' Java Creators Program, where I freelanced developing games and experiences for

one of the most recognized platforms in the world.

Two years ago I took that foundation and built something entirely on my own, 'Cauldron's Rift',

a game I developed independently and successfully shipped to Steam. That experience

sharpened every skill I had: architecture, problem solving, shipping real products in a real environment.

Today I bring the same commitment to enterprise software. I specialize in Angular and .NET,

building clean, scalable, and maintainable solutions, with additional expertise in Blazor.

I don't just write code that works. I write code that lasts.

Open To

I'm open to all opportunities, freelance contracts, full-time roles, and collaborative projects alike.

Whether it's a startup that needs a product built from the ground up, or an

established team looking for a focused .NET and

Angular developer, I'm interested in the

conversation.

I bring the same level of commitment to every project, regardless of size or scope.

Beyond Code

Outside of professional development, I'm currently leading

a large-scale Minecraft project alongside eight other leads,

each managing our own teams and responsibilities to

bring an ambitious minigame to life.

It's as much about coordination and

leadership as it is about the creation itself.

When I'm not building something, I'm spending time with

my family. I've also recently developed an interest in cooking, and it's only a matter

of time before that becomes another thing I go too deep on.
