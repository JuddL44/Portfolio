About Me

Software Development

Nine years ago, I wrote my first line of code after being inspired by a book about pixel art.

What began as a passion for game development evolved into a professional career, earning a place in Mojang

Studios' Java Creators Program, where I freelanced developing experiences for

one of the most recognized games in the world.

Two years ago I took that foundation and built something entirely on my own, 'Cauldron's Rift',

a game I developed independently and successfully shipped to Steam. That experience

sharpened every skill I had: architecture, problem solving, and shipping real products for consumers.

Today I bring the same commitment to enterprise software. I specialize in Angular and .NET,

building clean, scalable, and maintainable solutions, with additional expertise in Blazor.

I don't just write code that works. I write code thats scalable, and lasts.

Open To

I'm open to all opportunities, freelance contracts, full-time roles, and collaborative projects alike.

Whether it's a startup that needs a product built from the ground up, or an

established team looking for a focused .NET and Angular developer,

I'm interested in the conversation.

I bring the same level of commitment to every project, regardless of size or scope.

Beyond Code

Outside of professional development, I'm currently leading

a large-scale project for Minecraft Realms alongside eight other leads,

each managing our own teams and responsibilities to bring this project to life.

It's as much about coordination and leadership as it is about the creation itself.
